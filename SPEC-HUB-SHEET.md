# The hub and the sheet, extracted

From the supplied concepts. The palette is named in the artwork and measured
in DESIGN.md; everything below is layout.

## The world

Warm. Parchment ground, white cards, stone hairlines, charcoal ink. A gold
`d20` crest sits centred in the header of both screens and is the brand mark.
Serif display for names; sans for everything read. Ornamental corner flourishes
on the outer frame, and a small centred flourish as a closing divider.

## What this changes

- The palette moves from cool grey to **warm parchment**. Every neutral changes.
- Both screens are **card-on-ground**, not full-bleed lists.
- A **crest** and a **level shield** are new marks; both are SVG.
- The sheet is **tabbed**, not one scroll — Overview / Combat / Inventory / Notes.
- The hub carries a **campaign** and an **activity feed**, neither of which the
  data model has yet. Activity can be read from the log; campaign cannot, and
  is stubbed until a room carries one.
